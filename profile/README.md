EasyWorship 7 build 7.4.0.7 is developed by Softouch, and it’s dedicated church presentation software built specifically around that workflow: song lyrics, Bible verses, sermon notes, and media, all organized into a live-ready service schedule rather than a fixed slide deck. Office 365 Pro Plus covers general office productivity for church administration, but EasyWorship is purpose-built for what happens on the actual screen during a live service, live song switching, scripture lookup, and multi-output display support PowerPoint simply isn’t designed for.

This 7.4 release, confirmed as the final stable build by EasyWorship’s own support team after a beta testing period, added direct SongSelect API integration from CCLI, letting you search and import song lyrics straight from the Songs tab instead of manually typing them in or copying from a separate source. For Premium subscribers, the Media tab also gained direct access to download new Premium media content without leaving the app. Scripture Search and Reflow rounds out the update: instead of dumping an entire Bible passage onto one crowded slide, Scripture Reflow automatically builds separate slides based on settings you define, keeping text readable at a distance.

The Stage Display, also called the Foldback Screen, is a genuinely thoughtful addition for worship teams specifically. It’s a stage-facing display showing the current lyrics along with a preview of the next slide’s opening line, functioning as a cheat sheet for musicians and vocalists who need to stay in sync without staring at a phone or printed lyric sheet mid-song. Multiple output support covers up to 3 separate outputs, each capable of showing different themes from the same underlying content, useful for running a stage display, a congregation-facing screen, and a livestream feed simultaneously from one service file.
